

# WRB Intro

## First exercise

_What topics of questions interest you? What would "success" be for you? What is your ideal research outcome?_

I want to better understand how the representations and cognitive processes that underlie language learning and language use are shaped by interactional (and more recently) social habits and motives. Success for me would be making significant progress on any aspect of this question, but especially those that I've already got good momentum on: psycholinguistics of conversation, documentation of input and early developmental patterns in wide-ranging communities, and how children learn "hard" words. My ideal research outcome is to concretely bridge the gap between language socialization and language acquisition research by interpreting ideas of social and cultural language use and learning into the language of cognitive psychology.
