I am a first-year PhD student in the Comparative Human Development department. Before coming to Chicago, I worked as the lab manager for the Social & Developmental Cognitive Neuroscience lab under Dr. Rebecca Saxe at MIT. And I graduated from Wellesley College with degrees in Cognitive & Linguistic Sciences and Spanish at Wellesley College. I am broadly interested in the ethnolinguistic environments in which children learn language, especially within multilingual and multicultural contexts.
