### About me

<br>
I joined the Chatter Lab in summer 2023 after graduating from the University of Pennsylvania with majors in Linguistics and Computer Information Science. During undergrad, I worked primarily with Dr. Delphine Dahan to study how communicative goals drive the interpretation of utterances in everyday conversation. Here at the Chatter Lab, I'm involved in projects investigating the co-development of interactional and linguistic competence in children through experimental and observational methods. My primary research interest is in the development of pragmatic reasoning in the context of naturalistic language production and comprehension.
