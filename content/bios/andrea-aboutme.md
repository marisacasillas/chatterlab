I am a third-year undergrad at the University of Chicago majoring in Neuroscience and Linguistics. I joined the Chatter Lab in December 2025. Broadly, I’m interested in how children’s early environments shape language development at both the behavioral and neural levels. I hope to explore how linguistic input, social context, and cognitive processes interact to support language learning, and I’m thrilled to grow alongside the lab as I deepen my research experience!
